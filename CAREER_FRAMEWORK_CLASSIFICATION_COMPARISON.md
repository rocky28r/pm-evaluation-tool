## Reading the Change

- The former model emphasized individual PM craft, especially feature specification, delivery, and quality assurance.
- The new model keeps that craft visible while separating problem framing, delivery quality, and outcome learning.
- AI is treated as a judgment and operating-practice expectation rather than as a stand-alone tool proficiency.
- Leadership roles shift from detailed feature execution toward portfolio decisions, product operating models, talent development, and cross-functional influence.
- Principal remains an expert individual-contributor role; Group Product Manager and Director Product carry explicit people and organizational leadership accountability.
